Arkhe Guard

An AI agent on NEAR can only spend what its owner signed.

① function-call key
② owner-signed mandate
③ anchored evidence log

What this does not prove

  • That the model reasoned well. It bounds what a fooled model can spend.
  • That the owner's mandate is wise. The owner decides that.
  • That an operator logged everything. On-chain events are the independent record of spending.

Arkhe Guard

It doesn't make the model smarter. It makes a fooled model harmless.
